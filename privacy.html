Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy describes how information may be handled when using SiteInspect, a construction inspection and punch-list application.

Information you provide

Project records may include inspection notes, locations, photographs, annotations, report details, and collaborator information when those features are used.

Permissions and device data

If you grant camera or photo library permissions, SiteInspect can use them to attach photos to inspection records. You control which permissions you grant through your device settings.

Storage and sharing

Project information may be stored on your device. Where iCloud synchronization or collaboration is available and enabled, information may be processed through Apple's services and shared with participants you authorize. Apple's services are subject to their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

You can remove project information using available app controls. Shared copies or cloud backups may persist separately. For data questions or deletion assistance, contact support.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

SiteInspect is designed for professional use and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated; changes will be published on this page.
